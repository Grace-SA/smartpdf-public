Smart PDF Number Sans Regular is a renamed ASCII subset of Smart PDF Sans Regular, itself derived from Google Fonts Noto Sans SC at commit 2894aab31764f10f29c421bdfd2340d3b382d384. Only ASCII 32-126 is retained, weight 400, four-byte glyph padding, no optional layout features. Build with scripts/prepare-number-font.py. Original copyright and SIL OFL 1.1 are preserved in OFL.txt. No external font requests or user fonts are used.
